# Data sources

## `magnesium_ami.csv`
Study-level event counts for intravenous magnesium in acute myocardial infarction, extracted from:
- Teo KK, Yusuf S, Collins R, et al. Effects of intravenous magnesium in suspected acute myocardial infarction: overview of randomised trials. BMJ. 1991;303(6816):1499-1503.
- Li J, Zhang Q, Zhang M, Egger M. Intravenous magnesium for acute myocardial infarction. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2007;(2):CD002755.
- ISIS-4 Collaborative Group. ISIS-4: a randomised factorial trial assessing early oral captopril, oral mononitrate, and intravenous magnesium sulphate in 58,050 patients with suspected acute myocardial infarction. Lancet. 1995;345(8951):669-685.

Era classification (pre-thrombolysis / transition / thrombolysis) follows the standard narrative of the magnesium meta-analysis literature.

## `statins_hf_obs.csv` and `statins_hf_rct.csv`
Study-level hazard ratios and summary statistics for statins in heart failure, extracted from:
- Mozaffarian D, et al. (Circulation 2004)
- Horwich TB, et al. (J Card Fail 2004)
- Go AS, et al. (JAMA 2006)
- Foody JM, et al. (J Card Fail 2006)
- Anker SD, et al. (Cochrane / pooled analysis, 2006)
- CORONA Study Investigators. Rosuvastatin in older patients with systolic heart failure. N Engl J Med. 2007;357(22):2248-2261.
- GISSI-HF Investigators. Effect of rosuvastatin in patients with chronic heart failure (the GISSI-HF trial). Lancet. 2008;372(9645):1231-1239.

These files contain aggregate published data only. Individual patient data were not used.
